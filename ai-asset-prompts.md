## Usage Tips

**For Midjourney:** Append `--ar 1:1 --s 250 --v 6.1` for icons, `--ar 9:19 --v 6.1` for phone screenshots

**For DALL-E 3:** Use "I NEED to test how the tool works with extremely simple prompts. DO NOT add detail, just use it AS-IS:" before the prompt to prevent DALL-E from over-embellishing

**For Ideogram:** Add "graphic design, vector, flat" to keep it clean. Ideogram handles text well if you need any

**Color Consistency:** After generating, adjust colors in Figma/Photoshop to match the exact hex values. AI generators approximate colors but rarely nail exact hex codes.

**Export Checklist:**
- App icon: 1024x1024 PNG, no alpha channel, no rounded corners (iOS adds them)
- Splash icon: PNG with transparency, 200px wide
- Adaptive icon foreground: 1024x1024 PNG with transparency
- Favicons: Export at 32x32 and 16x16, also generate .ico
- Coach avatars: 512x512 PNG, crop to circle in-app
- Store assets: Follow exact Apple/Google size requirements
